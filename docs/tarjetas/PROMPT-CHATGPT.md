# Prompt para ChatGPT: tarjeta de presentación

Para pedirle a ChatGPT (generador de imágenes) una versión o variaciones de
la tarjeta. Sube junto con el prompt el logo (`docs/tarjetas/logo.png`) y, si
quieres que se parezca a la nuestra, `docs/tarjetas/vista/mockup.png`.

Ojo: el QR que dibuja ChatGPT **no funciona**, es un dibujo. Para imprimir se
usan los PDF de `docs/tarjetas/imprenta/`, que tienen el QR real.

---

```text
Design a premium, minimal business card for my digital agency, Judo Marketing (Miami). Show the FRONT and the BACK side by side as a realistic mockup on a dark surface, photographed from slightly above, with soft studio light.

MATERIAL: black PVC plastic card, standard business card size (3.5 x 2 in), rounded corners, matte finish with a subtle sheen. The black background is the plastic itself, so keep it pure deep black, no gradients or glows on the background.

BRAND: deep black (#0A0A0F), electric purple (#7B2DFF), light purple (#A855F7), soft white (#F5F5F7). Fonts: Poppins SemiBold/Bold for headlines and the name, Inter for everything else. High contrast, clean, futuristic but serious. Lots of empty space. Nothing cheap, no clip art, no stock-photo look.

USE THE ATTACHED LOGO exactly as it is (white 3D "JUDO" wordmark made of concentric lines, with "marketing" underneath). Do not redraw it or change the letters.

FRONT (the promise and the offer):
- The logo large on the left half.
- Right side, right-aligned: "Your business," in white and "in good hands." in light purple (#A855F7), Poppins Bold. Under it, a short thin purple line (#7B2DFF).
- Under the line, four services in small light gray, one per line, right-aligned: "Websites & E-commerce", "Delivery & Ordering Apps", "Automations", "Business Advisory".
- Background detail: thin dark-purple broken concentric lines rippling out from the logo, like a fingerprint (a hand that takes care of the business), very subtle, tone on tone.

BACK (the person, very clean, lots of air, no icons):
- Left column in three groups with generous space between them:
  top: "Junior Osorio" (Poppins SemiBold, white), under it "DIRECTOR" in small letter-spaced light purple capitals;
  middle: "Complex problems, solved." in light gray with "solved." in light purple;
  bottom: "+1 305-497-9266" (Poppins SemiBold, the most visible contact) and under it in small light gray "judomarketing.net · Miami · EN / ES".
- Right side, vertically centered: a QR code on a white rounded square (placeholder), under it "See our work →" in white with a purple arrow.
- A few subtle dark-purple concentric lines only in the bottom right corner, the same fingerprint motif as the front.
- No email address.

All text must be spelled exactly as written above. Keep at least 5.5 mm (0.22 in) of empty margin from every edge; nothing may touch or crowd the edges. Text must be crisp and readable, minimum 6 pt.
```

---

**Si quieres variaciones**, agrega al final una de estas líneas:

- `Make a version with the logo in metallic silver foil and "in good hands." in purple foil.`
- `Make a vertical version (portrait), same content.`
- `Make a version where the front only has the logo centered and the tagline under it.`
